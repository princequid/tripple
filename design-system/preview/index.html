Approved palette

A-16 · A-17

Crimson leads interaction and brand; white carries pages and galleries; soft grey carries neumorphic controls; charcoal carries text. Supporting accents stay secondary to crimson. Contrast values are against the background unless stated (T-15 v2.1).
